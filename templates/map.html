Every note is a dot floating in a 3D cloud. Lines are the real links between notes — solid where a note names another with a [[link]], faint where two notes are just about the same thing. Bigger dots are more connected. Drag the background to spin the cloud, scroll to zoom, drag a dot to pull it, click a dot to read the note. Use 3D/2D to flatten it.
