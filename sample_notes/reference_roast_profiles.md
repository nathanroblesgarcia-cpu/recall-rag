# Reference: roast profiles

Notes Maria and I keep on how we roast.

- First crack usually starts around 196 degrees Celsius. That is the signal the
  roast is nearly there.
- For a medium roast we drop the beans at about 210 degrees, just after first
  crack settles, for a sweet, balanced cup.
- For espresso we go a touch darker and slower to build body.
- Rest roasted beans at least 3 days before brewing so the gas settles.
