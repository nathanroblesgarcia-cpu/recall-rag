# Reference: pour-over brew guide

Our standard pour-over recipe for the filter bar.

- Ratio: 1 gram of coffee to 16 grams of water (a 1:16 ratio). For a single cup
  that is about 20 g of coffee to 320 g of water.
- Water temperature: 96 degrees Celsius.
- Total brew time: about 3 minutes, in a slow spiral pour.
- Grind: medium, like coarse sea salt. Adjust finer if it brews too fast.
