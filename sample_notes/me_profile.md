# About me

I am Sam Carter, owner of Ember & Oak Coffee, a café and small-batch roastery in
Portland. I opened it in 2021 after years working as a barista.

I run the roasting side myself and manage the café with a small team. Most of my
day is hands-on: roasting, dialling in espresso, ordering green beans, and keeping
the books. I keep written notes on everything so nothing lives only in my head.
