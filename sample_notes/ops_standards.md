# Café standards

House rules so every drink tastes the same no matter who makes it.

- Standard espresso shot: 18 g of coffee in, 36 g of espresso out, in about 27
  seconds. If it runs too fast, grind finer; too slow, grind coarser.
- Steam milk to 65 degrees Celsius, no hotter. Scalded milk ruins a flat white.
- Cup sizes: small 6 oz, regular 8 oz, large 12 oz.
- Every barista tastes their first espresso of the day before serving.
